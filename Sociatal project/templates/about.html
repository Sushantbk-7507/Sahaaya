About Sahaaya

Sahaaya is a centralized web platform connecting individuals willing to provide assistance with verified social welfare organizations.

Many well-meaning citizens struggle to locate trustworthy orphanages, old-age homes, and community shelters because information across the web is scattered, unverified, or outdated. Sahaaya solves this gap through administrator-verified profiles, location-based discovery, itemized assistance requirements, and direct supporter-to-organization communication—without acting as a payment intermediary.

Core Principles
  • Mandatory Admin Verification
  • Transparent Itemized Requirements
  • Direct Call, Email & Map Navigation
  • Community Inaccuracy Reporting
Global Impact Alignment

UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

Sahaaya directly contributes to five United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 1
No Poverty

Connects community resources, essential supplies, and support directly with vulnerable communities and shelters.

SDG 2
Zero Hunger

Helps supporters immediately identify welfare centers requiring staple food grains, nutrition supplements, and meal supplies.

SDG 4
Quality Education

Supports access to educational materials, textbooks, notebooks, school bags, and stationery for children in care.

SDG 10
Reduced Inequalities

Improves visibility and equitable access to essential resources for disadvantaged seniors, children, and displaced individuals.

SDG 17
Partnerships for the Goals

Builds trusted, transparent partnerships connecting citizens, volunteers, academic institutions, and local welfare organizations.
