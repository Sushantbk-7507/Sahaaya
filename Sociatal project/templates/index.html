Transparent Workflow

How Sahaaya Works

Five structured stages ensure accuracy, accountability, and direct human connection without intermediaries.

1
Data Collection

Gather verified details, facilities, and itemized needs from welfare centers.

2
Admin Verification

Administrators audit addresses, phone numbers, and legitimacy before approval.

3
Portal Publishing

Only approved organizations become publicly discoverable on Sahaaya.

4
Search and Filter

Supporters filter by category, area, city, or pincode to find nearby centers.

5
Direct Support

Call, email, visit via map, or send a direct inquiry to coordinate help.
